Independent buildCUDA · NumPy · JAX

A transformer built from raw CUDA kernels

No PyTorch, TensorFlow or JAX in the core math. I wrote the kernels by hand (tiled matmul, warp shuffle softmax, tree reduction), built an autograd engine on top, then a full decoder transformer on top of that.

Then I trained the exact same model class on two unrelated problems. Once on TinyStories to generate text, once on MovieLens 1M to recommend the next film. The only change was what the tokens meant.

The recommender is deployed and live. 1.7M parameters, 4 decoder layers, 4 attention heads, 3,706 movie tokens.

BSc dissertationPyTorch · Flask

Sentinel, forensic vehicle identification

From one photo, Sentinel names a car's make, model and year, then pulls its Euro NCAP crash safety rating. It is the first stage of an accident reconstruction pipeline, so every prediction carries a calibrated confidence and flags itself for human review when it should.

Two ResNet50 branches read the whole car and a front crop (grille, headlights, badge). Ordinal regression handles the year, Kendall uncertainty weighting balances the three tasks, and temperature scaling makes the confidence scores honest. TorchScript cut inference from 480ms to 46ms.

Built in 10 staged experiments with ablations and failure mode analysis. Trained on Stanford Cars, 49 makes, 189 models, 16 model years, about 51M parameters.

Packing Party Gift Offer

Every year, groups across the UK fill Operation Christmas Child shoeboxes with gifts for children. Samaritan's Purse sells those gifts in bulk at a discount so each group's fundraising goes further. The whole thing ran on spreadsheets. I designed and built the replacement on my own.

Groups pick packs of 50 from hairbrushes, stationery, toy cars, dolls and more, watch a running total so they stay inside their budget, add the date of their packing party, and pay by card. A confirmation email goes out automatically. Behind the scenes the team watches stock on a live dashboard that flags items running low before they sell out.

It is live right now for the 2026 season, ahead of National Collection Week in November. A group can place a full order in about five minutes.

Startup buildDjango · React · LangChain

ExcelPoint, a study partner built on RAG

Students upload their own material, PDFs, Word documents, lecture videos or audio, and ExcelPoint turns it into something they can talk to. Ask a question and the answer comes from your notes, not the open internet. The chat remembers the whole conversation and picks up where you left off next time.

From the same material it generates quizzes, flashcards with spaced repetition, and adaptive learning paths that change as you progress. Progress tracking and achievements show what is sticking and what needs another look.

Heavy lifting runs in the background. Celery workers on Redis chunk and embed documents without blocking the app and retry failed jobs automatically. Responses are cached to cut model costs, storage switches between local disk and S3, and the API is protected with Google OAuth, JWT, role based access and rate limiting.

Independent buildPython · PostgreSQL

Payment authorisation simulator

A CLI first model of the real card payment path, merchant to acquirer to card network to issuer, with ISO 8583 response codes, soft and hard declines, acquirer routing, 3DS as a measurable decision, and chargebacks that arrive 2 to 12 weeks late, the way real fraud labels do.

Global retry rules treat every bank the same. I learned behaviour profiles per issuer from historical outcomes and replaced the global rules with issuer aware retry and routing strategies. Every attempt is logged so each decision can be replayed and explained.

National HighwaysspaCy · NetworkX · LLMs

A knowledge graph over engineering manuals

National Highways runs on thousands of technical documents that cite, replace and depend on each other. Nobody could see that web. I was the sole developer and researcher on an internal tool that reads the PDFs, recognises document codes with a custom spaCy pipeline, classifies how documents relate, and stores the result as a graph engineers can query through an LLM assistant.

It answers questions like which documents depend on this standard, which ones replaced it, and which ones sit isolated with almost no links. The full source stays internal.
